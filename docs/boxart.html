CatSynth 64, v1.0, box art

Straight off an eighties shelf

The CatSynth 64 box as it would have been printed in the mid-eighties: one black cat against a spotlight, period lettering, faded inks on yellowed card and a strip of screen shots on the back. Here are the front cover, the full box laid flat, and the disk that goes inside.
